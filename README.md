## What is Domain Rating?

Domain Rating (DR) is a 0–100 score made by Ahrefs that shows how strong a website’s backlink
profile is compared with every other website. The more unique, trusted websites link to a site with
followed links, the higher its DR. SEOs and founders use it to judge websites before buying a domain,
guest posting or building links, and to track their own site’s growth.

| DR | Level | What it usually means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–9 | Just starting | New sites with few links from other websites |
| 10–29 | Sprouting | Early traction: a handful of good backlinks |
| 30–49 | Growing | A solid, growing site with real links |
| 50–69 | Established | A well-known site in its niche |
| 70–89 | Authority | Major brands, publishers and big platforms |
| 90–100 | Elite | The strongest sites on the web |
